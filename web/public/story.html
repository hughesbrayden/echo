01 The problem

For blind and low-vision people, a cane only tells part of the story.

A cane finds what's on the ground about a step ahead. Everything higher, or a few steps further, arrives without warning.

The question

How might we tell the rest of the story, without expensive specialist hardware?

02 The solution

Echo, a lookout you can wear.

Echo is an agentic navigation system built for video glasses. As you walk, it tells you what's in your way and how to get around it, and stays quiet the rest of the time.

How to use it
  1. Put it on.Video glasses at eye level. For this walk, a phone stands in.
  2. Start Echo.Set your eye height, tap Start and allow the camera and motion sensors.
  3. Walk.Silence means nothing needs you. Two chimes and "Stop." mean wait.
Some of what it said
  • "Door, about 5 steps ahead."
  • "Person, about 5 steps ahead. Bear right."
  • "A step, about 4 steps ahead. Slow down."
